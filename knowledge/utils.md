- scenario: ToUint32 reads leading digits and ignores the rest (`127536abc` gives 127536), returns 0 for `abc123` and empty input, wraps `-1` to the maximum and saturates `999999999999`; ToUint64 does the same at 64 bits; ToFloat gives 0 for `abc1.0`. IsInteger accepts ` -10` and rejects ` 1 0`, `1.0`, `0x1`, `120 h`, a lone space and empty; IsNaturalNumber accepts ` 10`. | check: converting user, NFO or add-on strings to numbers holds for negative, overflowing, trailing-garbage and blank input as the helper actually answers | at: `xbmc/utils/StringUtils.cpp:StringUtils::ToUint32`, `xbmc/utils/StringUtils.cpp:StringUtils::IsInteger`
- scenario: AlphaNumericCompare orders embedded numbers numerically (`2` before `12`) and must cope with 20-digit runs. Nordic collation for `nor`, `nob`, `nno` and `dan` is z, æ, ø, å with ä and ö folded onto æ and ø; for `swe` and `fin` z, å, ä, ö; case weighs the same and other languages get no override. FindWords finds word starts in CJK (`我的视频`) and after non-ASCII letters; utf8_strlen counts full-width text in characters. | check: sorting or text search changes hold for numbers past 64 bits, Nordic letters under a Nordic and a non-Nordic language, and CJK or accented input | at: `xbmc/utils/StringUtils.cpp:StringUtils::AlphaNumericCompare`, `xbmc/utils/StringUtils.cpp:StringUtils::GetNordicCollationWeight`
- convention: CRegExp patterns use non-capturing groups that must not be counted (`S(\d+)E(\d+)(?:[^\\/]*)$` has two captures), named groups (`(?<first>a)`), optional groups left unmatched (`(a)(b)?(c)?`), case-insensitive construction and Unicode patterns (`Бог!$`, `\x{00E0}`) that need UTF-8 mode; capture indexes past the count are bounds-checked. | check: a new regex (advancedsettings patterns, episode or title matching) is checked for capture numbering with non-capturing groups and for Unicode input | at: `xbmc/utils/RegExp.cpp:CRegExp::RegComp`, `xbmc/utils/RegExp.cpp:CRegExp::GetMatch`
- scenario: BCP 47 tags come with extlang (`ar-aao`), 4-letter scripts (`en-Afak`), 2-letter and 3-digit regions (`en-GB`, `es-419`, `en-000`), variants (`en-1606nict-1694acad`), extensions (`ab-a-bc-d-ef`), private use (`x-a-bcd`), grandfathered tags (`i-ami`, `cel-gaulish`), mixed case and padding; canonicalization replaces deprecated codes (`iw-Hebr-IL` to `he-Hebr-IL`, `en-BU` to `en-MM`, `mo-DD` to `ro-DE`). | check: code that splits a language tag on `-` or assumes 2-letter regions holds for a numeric region, a script, an extlang and a grandfathered tag | at: `xbmc/language/i18n/Bcp47Parser.cpp:CBcp47Parser::Parse`, `xbmc/language/i18n/Bcp47.cpp:CBcp47::Canonicalize`
