- convention: Archive and disc paths carry the URL-encoded container path as hostname and the inner path after it: `zip://smb%3a%2f%2fhost%2fpath%2fmovie.zip/movie.mkv`, `rar://D%3A%2FMovies%2Fmovie.rar/movie.mkv` (either hex case, `/` or `%5c`), `archive://...movie.tar.gz/BDMV/index.bdmv`. - convention: A stack is `stack://` plus members joined by ` , `: `stack:///movies/movie/cd1/file1.avi , /movies/movie/cd2/file2.avi`, `stack://D:\movies\part_1.avi , D:\movies\part_2.avi`. Members can be UNC, smb, nfs, special or http, or disc structures in `disc 1`, `disc 2` folders, mixed (`disc 1/BDMV/index.bdmv , disc 2/VIDEO_TS/VIDEO_TS.IFO , disc 3/part a/movie.mkv`).
